Ride the Line. Send the Jumps. Conquer the Mountain.

Get ready for a high-speed mountain biking challenge where skill, timing, and nerve are everything.

Take control of your rider and race through demanding mountain courses packed with big jumps, sweeping berms, technical rock gardens, fast downhill flow trails, and heart-pounding drops. Build speed through the corners, choose the perfect line through rough terrain, and keep your bike under control when the trail gets wild.

Every section tests a different part of your riding. Pump through berms to carry momentum, pick your way through unforgiving rocks, launch off jumps and stick the landing, then let it rip down smooth, twisting flow trails. Hesitate at a drop and you’ll lose precious time. Hit it too fast and you might find yourself flying over the bars.

Your goal is simple: clear the entire course and reach the finish line.

Master each trail, improve your times, and prove you have what it takes to become the ultimate downhill rider.

Features:

* Fast, skill-based mountain bike gameplay
* Jumps with challenging takeoffs and landings
* High-speed berms and flowing corners
* Technical rock gardens that demand precise bike control
* Downhill flow-trail sections built for speed
* Drops that test your commitment and timing
* Challenging courses with multiple terrain types
* Replayable runs where finding the fastest line makes all the difference

Choose your line. Commit to the drop. Keep the wheels down.
